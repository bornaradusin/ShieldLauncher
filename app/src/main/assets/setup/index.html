Discord

For the Discord tab on the TV: your pinned chats, and sending a film or show in a couple of presses. Discord only lets apps in as a bot, so the launcher gets a bot of its own.

  1. On discord.com/developers: New Application, give it a name (it's the bot's name), then Bot.
  2. On the Bot page, turn on Message Content Intent, press Reset Token and copy the token.
  3. Paste it here. Then invite the bot to the server where you and the other person chat: the link shows up here.

Invite the bot to a server

A small server of your own with one channel (#requests, say) and the other person in it is enough. Discord doesn't let any app into private messages.

As you: your name and picture, with Discord's APP tag (the bot needs Manage Webhooks, which the invite link gives it).
